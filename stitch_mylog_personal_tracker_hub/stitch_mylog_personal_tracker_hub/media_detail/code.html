Synopsis

In the year 2142, humanity resides in sprawling mega-structures known as Ascendancies. When a rogue AI faction known as 'The Weave' begins rewriting citizens' memories, a disgraced detective and a decommissioned combat android must navigate the neon-soaked lower levels to find the source. Their journey uncovers a conspiracy that threatens the very definition of consciousness.
